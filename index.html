📖 The Story

Your Grandfather's Legacy

Your grandfather Alaric left you a weathered envelope and a single rusted key. Inside — the deed to an abandoned farm in a village you've never heard of: MeloSarisa.

Trading your city cubicle for muddy boots, you arrive to find the farm in ruins, the soil shimmering with strange light, and the villagers guarding secrets older than the mountains themselves.

Every seed you plant draws you deeper into the mystery. The crystals that grow wild through the earth pulse with an energy the village council refuses to discuss. Something sleeps beneath the old mine — and it's starting to wake.

40+ hours of main story. 100+ hours of side quests, secrets, and discoveries. Your choices shape MeloSarisa's fate.
